*Rani Rahav and Ronit Arbel at a summit meeting, Boaz Ben Zion and Maya Buskila split up, and wedding season reaches Keshet: "The Bottom Line," an industry gossip column*

*Third-party coverage, not Nimrod Dvir's writing. English translation of the Hebrew original by nrg Maariv staff, published in nrg on November 15, 2010. [Read the original in Hebrew](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/nrg/online/1/ART2/178/322.html).*

*Rani Rahav and Ranit Arbel at the Time Out 8th anniversary event (Gal Uchovsky)*

Two months were all it took for Boaz Ben-Zion and Maya Buskila to split up.

A moment after [we reported](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/nrg/online/1/ART2/175/562.html) on the contacts between Eyal Golan and Rani Rahav over a collaboration, Rahav and Ronit Arbel met at Time Out's 8th anniversary party. Gal Uchovsky couldn't resist and documented the moment.

Mark Zuckerberg versus Pini Siluk: shut down his Facebook. Siluk, in response, opened a new account.

More Facebook business: Yossi Elias and Rinat Klein, CEO of HOT Bidur Yisraeli, in a catfight over the credibility of "Beauty and the Geek."

Television editor Asaf Greenbaum was surprised on Saturday night when his partner, Moshik Israel of the Big Brother casting team, threw him a surprise party at their home. Among those present: model Chen Shiloni and her new husband, Livat Greenbaum, deputy editor of entertainment news, Nili Beitel of "Big Brother," Keren Ohayon, Meir Eliel, documentarian for "Reshet," and Arbel Zirlin of "The Amazing Race" and many more.

<!-- Source image not embedded; see source-record.json for provenance and licensing. -->

*Yoni Dotan (Amir Meiri)*

Wedding season 1: Avsha Agassi married his partner Dassi. Among those who came to congratulate: Zeevik Dror and the women of his office, Sefi Shaked, Danny Levy, Raz Shechnik, Yaron Lichtenstein, Yoni Dotan, Keren Natanzon, Liel Danir, Moshe Peretz and many fine others. In the artistic segment: singer Netanel and Moran Marciano, Agassi's good friend, in a special clip featuring a performance of a Shlomo Artzi song.

Wedding season 2: Ronit Maimon, chief referent at Keshet, received a marriage proposal from her partner of the past year, Guma Ravid. Her close friends from the franchisee skipped the broadcast of "MasterChef" and went out to celebrate with her.

Those who didn't make it to Avsha's wedding party were at Shlomi Laufer's on Friday evening, as he celebrated a birthday and a housewarming for the price of one. In attendance: Ran Lior (formerly Limor Livnat's spokesman), Ofer Vardi and Amit Leventhal of "Israel Hayom," Amit Kotler and Neri Brenner of ynet, Nimrod Dvir of Maariv, Yaron Frost of "Yedioth Ahronoth," Dvir Bar of Anani Communications, Lee Gat, Silvan Shalom's spokeswoman, Tzach Saar, spokesman for MK Alex Miller, Arik Elad, spokesman for MK Moti Matalon, Michael Dolinger of "yes" and of course, Yardena Arazi of "Café Telad." Ron Granot of "Calcalist," on the other hand, arrived right on time: the following day.

Soon I'll go far: Hadas Rivak has gone to study in Germany and is expected back soon.
