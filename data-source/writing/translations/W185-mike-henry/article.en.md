*The actor Mike Henry, who is in fact white, is the man behind the character of Cleveland, the star of the animated series The Cleveland Show. In an exclusive interview he explains the difference between his series and Family Guy, talks about the collaboration with Kanye West, and about what it's like to voice an African-American character*

*English translation of the Hebrew original by Nimrod Dvir, published in nrg Maariv on March 30, 2011. [Read the original in Hebrew](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/nrg/online/47/ART2/227/227.html).*

"It's very cool to be interviewed by an Israeli paper," says Mike Henry (47), the actor and voice artist behind the character of Cleveland Brown, the star of the successful animated series The Cleveland Show (Saturdays at 6:50 p.m. on Yes Comedy). "The Cleveland Show is written mainly by Jews, so it's very exciting."

*I'm not a racist person. Mike Henry (publicity)*

The series, already in its third season in the United States (and its second in Israel), is a spin-off of the successful Family Guy. The series follows Cleveland Brown, an African-American who starts a new life with his son and a new partner. "When the decision came down to make a spin-off of Family Guy, I told myself the time had really come. I was very excited," says Henry. "From an early stage it was clear to me that Cleveland had a lot more to offer than what was seen on Family Guy. He was, to my mind, the perfect choice for a spin-off. We didn't want to make another Family Guy. We wanted something that would feel different."

**What's the difference?**

"We're nicer. The Cleveland Show isn't as mean as Family Guy. Yes, we make fun of celebrities and we have wild humor, but basically the show is aimed at a family audience. It's a sweet show with a less crazy vibe."

**How does a white man stand behind a Black character and series?**

"I created the character while we were working on Family Guy, so naturally I voice him too. I do a lot of characters, Black, white, women, perverts, everything. I'm not a racist person, so I don't worry that someone will criticize me. It works the other way too. We have an actor in the cast, Kevin Michael Richardson, who is Black, who plays Cleveland's son and also voices white characters. We aren't worried about it. As long as whoever does the voice is funny, it doesn't matter to me whether he's Black or white. I think animation is freer that way."

*A sweet show. The Cleveland Show (publicity)*

**One of the high points was Kanye West's guest appearance on the show.**

"Right. We heard Kanye was a fan of the show and asked him to come. He responded immediately and said yes. He came twice, and was really interested in the whole process. On our show he made fun of South Park after they made fun of him. He was very professional."

**Cleveland travels the world over the course of the show. What about a trip to Israel?**

"That could be fun. I'm sure we'll get there at some point. We're in the middle of the third season and we've reached Yemen, Hawaii, and other places. Maybe later on we'll get to Israel."

**How does the division of labor work with Seth MacFarlane, the creator of Family Guy?**

"The series is my responsibility, and Seth and I supervise all the scripts. I direct the voice acting and I have creative say over every episode. Of course you have to take into account notes from the network, which in most cases are fairly reasonable. Seth is involved in Family Guy and leaves The Cleveland Show in our care. We make the show we want to make. He doesn't have time to deal with it."

**Are you planning to stay in animation?**

"I love animation because within it you can go anywhere. You can 'get away cheap' with a lot of things because it's animation. For example, on Family Guy I voice a pedophile character. In animation you can present serious things as farce, and nobody takes us too seriously. But in the future I want to act in other things too. You could say I 'fell' into animation. I made 'regular' films until I met Seth and joined. In the future I definitely want to act in real films."
