## Source problems

- **¶5, ¶14, ¶15 and ¶19** `היכולות המיסטית`, `כשהגיעה הזמן`, `מאוחר היא תסביר` and `ה־הפוסיקט דולז` are small agreement, truncation or repeated-prefix errors. Their meaning is clear in context.
- **¶8, compared with ¶¶50–51** As the source record notes, the caption presents an alleged admission as a fact. The body calls the purported interview likely fabricated and records her manager’s denial. The inconsistency is already in the original.
- **¶23** The source record confirms the page 56 reference belongs to the print edition. The thing it points to is grammatically ambiguous, as noted in the table.
