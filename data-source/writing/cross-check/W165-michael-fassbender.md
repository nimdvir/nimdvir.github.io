## Source problems

- In ¶2, the phrasing can suggest that both the kidnapping and enslavement happened in the American South. Northup’s account places the kidnapping in Washington, D.C., and his enslavement in Louisiana. The geography problem comes from the source. [Library of Congress edition of Northup’s memoir](https://www.loc.gov/item/13004011/)
- In ¶13, `התאמנתי על הרבה על הצלפה` repeats a preposition. The intended sense is that Fassbender practiced using the whip a lot.
- In ¶18, `יחס אנושי` can mean human treatment or humane treatment. The sentence’s intended contrast is unclear. The English uses the broader and plausible “the way people treat each other.”
- In ¶27, `סרט יפיפיה` is an agreement error. The source record notes that the answer in this paragraph had to be recovered from outside a paragraph tag.
