## Source problems

- **¶3** `להחזרת את הכבוד העצמי` mixes two grammatical constructions. The intended sense is to regain their self-respect.
- **¶6** `מטמטמת` is singular after wording that may name two causes. See the ambiguity in the table.
- **¶18** The source record identifies `גאה לעשות לחשוף` as an apparent typo. In context Carrey is proud to show different sides of himself.
