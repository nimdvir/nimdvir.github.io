# Blog, news, and publishing templates

Date: October 1, 2026. Base: `4c2a5a0e1570d67753dab4bddfee50157198381c`.

## Request and result

Nim requested blog and news sections similar to [al-folio](https://al-folio.github.io/), plus reusable templates. The site now has a News feed and Latest posts feed below the homepage introduction, a Blog index, a year-grouped News archive, full article layouts, and matching navigation links. Dated rows take inspiration from al-folio while retaining the current CCE-inspired typography, colors, themes, and controls.

The workspace already contained a partial implementation on `feat/blog-news-2026-10-01`. That work was inspected and completed rather than discarded. The production base matches the earlier published refresh.

## What changed

- `src/content/blog/` supplies blog posts, and `src/content/news/` supplies announcements. Shared helpers filter and sort entries consistently across the homepage, indexes, detail routes, and related posts.
- The homepage displays up to three published entries of each type. Blog lists are newest first, with summaries, optional categories/topics, and approximate reading times. Full blog pages support Markdown, optional cover images, captions, bylines, and related posts.
- News can be a brief inline announcement with an optional linked title, or a full announcement with a detail page. The archive groups entries by publication year.
- The first news item records the actual October 1 website refresh. The blog is ready for Nim's first new post and currently shows a clear empty state. No new essay was invented or published under Nim's name.
- Existing journalism and interviews remain under Media. Four earlier unverified essays remain unpublished; their compatibility redirects remain in place.
- The shared stylesheet handles the new lists and articles, mobile stacking, and the wider navigation. A code-block background fix keeps syntax highlighting legible in light mode. The unlisted design reference includes the new layout specimens.

## Templates and authoring

The [authoring guide](../templates/README.md) explains how to copy a template, edit the fields and Markdown, review, and publish through a feature branch and pull request.

| Template | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Blog post](../templates/blog-post.md) | Full article with summary, topics, and optional cover image |
| [Short news](../templates/news-short.md) | Brief announcement and optional destination link |
| [Full news](../templates/news-long.md) | Announcement with its own page and body text |

All templates start with `draft: true`. Omitting the flag also defaults to a draft. Drafts do not generate public pages or appear in lists, related posts, or the sitemap. Because this is a public repository, committed source drafts are still readable on GitHub; the guide makes that distinction explicit.

Future-dated entries remain hidden at build time using the New York calendar date. This is not a scheduling service: a new build is required on or after the date. Published entries require a valid ISO calendar date and descriptive alt text when a cover image is supplied.
