## Editorial decisions

- Costco, Dexcom, Barrier Free Living, Nasher, Golf Buddy, and the INF 495 prototypes identify the advising/design context rather than implying an employer relationship.
- Golf Buddy retains its Adobe XD prototype without claiming a verified app-store release.
- Dexcom describes proposed UX improvements without representing them as validated clinical functionality.
- ClaimFame’s role and dates are supported by the September 2026 CV.
- Time Out was predominantly visual; its case study stays focused on the available wireframe and content structure.
- Trackimo retains the original distinction between survey respondents and observed device owners.
- No quantitative business uplift was invented for the project cards.
