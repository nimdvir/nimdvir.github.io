UAlbany's [College of Emergency Preparedness, Homeland Security and Cybersecurity](https://www.albany.edu/cehc) (CEHC, for anyone who needs to breathe) is featuring its graduates on signs around campus. One of them is me: my face, very large, next to my degree, my teaching internship and the job I took after graduating.

The sign also quotes me:

> “I’m grateful for my experience at CEHC. From the start, I recognized UAlbany’s strength in its community. Everyone, from deans to professors to administrative staff, was incredibly supportive. Their assistance was pivotal in graduating with distinction and honors from CEHC and UAlbany.”

Thank you, CEHC, for the support all through the PhD, and now for the sign. Mostly for the support.
