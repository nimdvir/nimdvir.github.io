The Israeli poster for *Sex and the City 2* has the four women, the desert, a giant silver 2 and, up in the corner, a line from [the review Dana Darvinsky and I wrote for nrg](/articles/sex-and-the-city-2/): “עושה את העבודה בצורה נפלאה.” Does the job wonderfully.
