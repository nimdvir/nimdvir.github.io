My PhD in Computer and Information Science from the University at Albany was conferred in November 2022. This month I finally walked at Commencement, floppy velvet cap and all.

A week later, UAlbany posted a video of its favorite moments from Commencement 2023, and I'm one of them: eyes shut, grinning, and being hugged by the one and only [Ella Yaari](https://www.instagram.com/ellaforesti/). You can [watch it on UAlbany's Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/universityatalbany/videos/ualbany-commencement-2023/996738048156801/).

Years of research and a whole dissertation, and the moment that made the university's highlight reel was a hug. Honestly, that seems about right.
