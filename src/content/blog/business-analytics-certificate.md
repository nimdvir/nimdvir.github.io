I direct the Undergraduate Certificate in Business Analytics at UAlbany's Massry School of Business, and I've put together [a page that explains it](/business-analytics-certificate/): the courses, when to take them, and how to apply.

The short version: five courses, 15 credits, covering spreadsheet analysis in Excel, statistics and regression, databases and SQL, and predictive analytics. Students who finish get a University at Albany certificate with their diploma and a notation on their transcript. Plenty of résumés say "data-driven." This one comes with paperwork.

## Who can apply

Any UAlbany major with a 3.25 GPA. To keep it, students need a B or better in every required course, so the designation means something.

One catch for students outside the business school: several of the required BITM courses restrict registration to declared business majors. Talk to your advisor before you plan the semesters.
