Pini Askal and Nimrod Dvir have been appointed deputies to Nirit Weiss, editor of the magazine "Pnai Plus." This follows the departure of the previous deputy editor, [Or Knispel](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/nrg/online/4/ART1/608/189.html).

Pini Askal, 28, serves as editor of Walla! Celebs, as the site's television critic and as a magazine editor. He will leave the site in a month. Nimrod Dvir has served over the past year as editor of the culture sections at "Pnai Plus."

Designer Amir Hasson, the man responsible for the design revolution at "Pnai Plus," is also leaving the magazine. Hasson is departing to study in London. Nadav Eliyahu, 34, senior graphic designer of the SBC group's magazines, will replace him in the role.

Nirit Weiss, editor of "Pnai Plus": I wish Or and Amir success on their new path and thank them for the wonderful and creative road we traveled together. Pini and Nadav are an excellent addition to the "Pnai Plus" staff, and I am sure their contribution to the magazine will be felt in the coming months."

Walla!'s vice president of content, Yehoram Dilmani, said in response: "Pini Askal is one of Walla!'s finest sons and he will always remain so; we wish him much success."
