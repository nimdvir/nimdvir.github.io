Ronald Rothfield, better known as DJ Raja Ram, has been in the business for close to 40 years. With his 70th birthday coming up, Ram isn't exactly resting: he still plays all over the world as one half of the trance duo Shpongle, with Simon Posford. Next weekend he lands here solo, this time for the Blu4Dance festival in Eilat.

"I must have been to Israel 20 or 30 times," he says by phone from his home in England. "I'm old, I've had the time. My first gig in Israel was in 1999 or 1998, at the Forum club in Beersheba. I've been coming back ever since. It's like my second home.

"I've been all over Israel. It's one of my favorite countries in the world. I love the attitude. People are friendly, everyone's laughing, sitting at tables outside, drinking coffee. It's a country with quality of life and calm. I'm Jewish myself, so I feel at home. It really is one of the great countries in the world."

**What can we expect from your set this time?**

"That's a hard question. With me it's never predictable, always a surprise. I'd love to make some magic with the people. It all comes from the crowd. It's a celebration, and we'll celebrate together, old friends and new. It's a wonderful invitation, to come to Eilat and play, and I'm looking forward to it. I'm expecting a riot of the senses, a feeling of happiness, beauty and purity. That's what we're hoping for."

**What's it like playing parties at 70?**

"As I got older I thought it would become impossible, that there'd be a gap between me and the people dancing. But I have to say I don't feel one. I've been doing this for 40 years, and it's a blessing from God that I still enjoy it. I can't explain why, because it's beyond logic. It's a great spot to be in, traveling the world and playing for people. It's a privilege.

"Music is a drug. It keeps you young and curious about the world. It gives you tremendous energy. The minute the party's over I'm off to Chile. I travel enormous distances and it really is tiring, but there's something in the air that makes you want to do it, and lets you. You dance, you sweat, you play. It's like playing football: there's a lot of pressure on you, but it's a huge amount of fun."

**You know it's hot where we are.**

"Of course. I know what to wear: sandals, shorts and a T-shirt. I've also got my own fan."
