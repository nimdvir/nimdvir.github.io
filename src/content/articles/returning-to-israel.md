It's madness here. After a strenuous week of work (Nirit cracked the whip over all of us), take a look at the plump issue that came out of our hands. Even in a week with no holiday or festival, the editorial team flexed its muscles and milked the very best out of itself: there are interesting interviews ([Amir Fay Guttman](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3254583,00.html) is handsome and knows it! [Hugh Jackman](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3254615,00.html) wants to be Clint Eastwood!), there are stormy reports (Tzach Oryan partied at the Eurovision, Amir Kaminer did a Bar-Zohar at the Cannes festival), there are grueling travel diaries ([Iris Abramovich](https://www.ynet.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3254644,00.html) joined the chubsters of "The Biggest Loser," and came back sweating), and there are colorful features too (Hadas Bashan, a quality girl, watches British television. Don't miss it). Plus the music and film sections, two gems as it is, are now getting a gust of energy and renewed strength (after all, a new film and music editor has to justify the salary). I wouldn't say there's a facelift coming, but maybe a shot of Botox to the T-zone.

And to think that just two weeks ago I was still in New York, doing morning runs between Urban Outfitters and Macy's. One phone call from the editor, another from the university, and that was that: back to Israel. Last week I was already standing in line at Burger Ranch, and a seven-year-old cut right around me. Note to self: the kids in Israel are tough.

Despite the hitches, so far coming back is the best part. So what if I have no apartment, no transportation, no money in the bank or, generally speaking, any sense of stability. And so what if you make beginner's mistakes, like going to the beach the day after landing and getting scorched like an American freha in the Tami Ben Ami look. So what. The main thing is that the energy is positive, the atmosphere is Zionist, and the Pnai Plus editorial office is more welcoming than ever (and when it isn't, you can always go hide with the girls at Rosh 1 and GO).

In any case, I hope the packed issues and the general atmosphere of madness help improve the weekend for all of us, and the landing for me. If not, I'm running back to New York with Dorit Shoval.

Nimrod Dvir

Incoming film and music editor

(and, in the very recent past, Pnai Plus correspondent in New York)
