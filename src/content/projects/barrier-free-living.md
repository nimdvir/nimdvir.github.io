## Context and my role

Barrier Free Living supports people with disabilities affected by domestic violence in New York City. I advised student content-strategy research examining its fundraising communication for 2020.

## Work completed

The team audited website content and interviewed staff and volunteers.

## Recommendations

The proposal covered donation-page content, video and social-media storytelling, readability, and accessibility. The report and presentation describe the recommendations.
