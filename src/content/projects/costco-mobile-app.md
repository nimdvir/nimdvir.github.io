## Context and my role

I advised the student team’s research and product design. The project considered checkout queues and the experience of finding products in a warehouse store.

## Work completed

The team developed a Scan & Go checkout concept and a Treasure Hunt feature with discounts and prizes. The materials include information architecture, screen mockups, a presentation, and an interactive prototype.

## Proposal

The prototype combines checkout and product-discovery features. It is a student concept; the materials below document the proposed design.
