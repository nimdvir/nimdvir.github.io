## From idea to definition

The original concept combined a GPS rangefinder with a club selector. Research expanded the idea to include real-time scoring, statistics, social connections, and course reviews.

## Design and development

The team used feedback from its professor and classmates to define the features and design the interface. The project followed discovery, definition, design, and development stages.

## Prototype

The final slide deck and Adobe XD prototype bring the proposed features and interface together into a navigable product concept.
