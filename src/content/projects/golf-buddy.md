## Context and my role

I advised this student product-design project. The initial idea combined a GPS rangefinder with a club selector.

## Work completed

The team developed the concept through research, feature definition, interface design, and feedback from the class. It expanded the proposed features to include scoring, statistics, social connections, and course reviews.
