## Research question

Does the amount of text on a landing page affect whether a visitor shares an email address?

## Methods

Two experiments in commercial settings compared landing pages with shorter and longer content. The study measured visitors’ willingness to provide an email address.

[![A long and a short version of a landing page side by side](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkndq6lyz/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto,w_1400,c_limit/v1795465093/PersonalSite/Portfolio/research/less-is-more/landing-pages.jpg)](https://res.cloudinary.com/dkndq6lyz/image/upload/f_auto,q_auto/v1795465093/PersonalSite/Portfolio/research/less-is-more/landing-pages.jpg)

## Findings

In these experiments, more content was associated with lower engagement. The results concern the pages and behavior studied; they do not establish that shorter text is preferable for every purpose.

## Paper

Dvir, N., & Gafni, R. (2018). [When Less Is More: Empirical Study of the Relation Between Consumer Behavior and Information Provision on Commercial Landing Pages](/publications/when-less-is-more/). *Informing Science*.
