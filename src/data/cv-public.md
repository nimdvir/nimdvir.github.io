## Research interests

My primary research agenda focuses on enhancing business performance through innovative applications of information technologies, with particular emphasis on the effective and efficient management of these technologies. This agenda centers on three interconnected objectives: (1) solving organizational and managerial problems through analytics and artificial intelligence; (2) examining the design, implementation, and use of information systems in applied and real-world environments; and (3) advancing research and pedagogy in artificial intelligence and machine learning.
